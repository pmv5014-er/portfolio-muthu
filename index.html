Project Details

Scotch Yoke Mechanism Implementation

Overview

This project involved designing, CAD modeling, and fabricating a functional prototype of a Scotch Yoke mechanism. A Scotch Yoke converts rotary motion into linear reciprocating motion (and vice-versa). It is commonly utilized in control valve actuators, high-pressure gas compressors, and some specialized internal combustion engines.

Key Accomplishments

  • 3D CAD Modeling: Developed full parameter-driven models for the crank, yoke, guide paths, and slider pin assembly in SolidWorks.
  • Kinematic Analysis: Conducted geometric simulations to verify velocity and acceleration profiles of the slider, proving pure sinusoidal motion.
  • 3D Printing Fabrication: Exported design components as STLs and configured 3D printer parameters (slicing density, shell thicknesses, PLA material cooling rates) to print and assemble a functional physical model demonstrating the conversion mechanism.

Technical Specifications

CAD Tools SolidWorks 2024
Fabrication Fused Deposition Modeling (FDM) / PLA Filament
Output Behavior Pure Simple Harmonic Motion (SHM)

Project Details

Portable Inlet Mounted Multistage Filter

Overview

Designed a portable, multi-layer radial water filtration unit meant to be mounted directly onto the inlet pipe of domestic overhead water tanks. This system solves a common household issue: impurities, sand, silt, and rust clogging piping and settling inside the overhead tank, necessitating frequent, tedious manual tank cleanings.

Key Accomplishments

  • Radial Multi-Layer Filtration: Engineered concentric filtration stages containing mesh screens, activated charcoal, and coarse sand aggregates to extract sediment and particles progressively.
  • Inlet Mounting Compatibility: Conceptualized a lightweight housing with quick-connect threads matching standard municipal/pump inlet pipes, making installation and removal simple.
  • Performance benefits: Successfully removed sand, silt, and heavy suspended impurities from incoming water supplies. Resulted in significantly cleaner overhead storage and extended plumbing appliance lifetimes.

Technical Specifications

Key Functions Particulate Filtration, Silt & Sediment Extraction
Mount Type Standard Inlet Gravity/Pressure Fit Threaded Connection
Media Layers Graded Mesh, Activated Carbon, Fine Aggregate
Project Details

Material-Based Performance Evaluation of Sprocket

Overview

This project focused on the structural integrity, stress distribution, and fatigue lifecycle analysis of power transmission sprockets. Using finite element analysis (FEA), the mechanical behavior of a typical chain drive sprocket was simulated under peak loads to identify failure points and compare the performance of different materials.

Key Accomplishments

  • FEA Setup & Meshing: Import SolidWorks geometry into ANSYS Workbench, apply localized grid refinement at the root of the sprocket teeth to capture high stress gradients accurately.
  • Static Structural Simulation: Modeled torque loadings, applying boundary constraints (fixed hubs and tangential forces at teeth interaction points) to extract Von Mises stresses and deformations.
  • Fatigue & Lifecycle Estimation: Evaluated stress-life curves (S-N curves) to compare structural steel, cast iron, and aluminum alloy. Identified the optimal material balancing weight, cost, and lifecycle duration.

Technical Specifications

Analysis Package ANSYS Static Structural & Fatigue Tool
Meshing Technique Octree Hex-dominant mesh with inflation layers at teeth fillets
Evaluated Materials Structural Steel, Grey Cast Iron, Aluminum 6061-T6
